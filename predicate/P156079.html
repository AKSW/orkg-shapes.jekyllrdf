Input Parameter

Input Parameter refers to the specific factors or variables that are used as inputs in the research study in order to analyze and assess the mineralogical and chemical composition as well as the distribution of rare earth elements in clay-rich sediments from Southeastern Nigeria. The identification and characterization of these input parameters are essential for understanding the research methodology and outcomes of the study.
