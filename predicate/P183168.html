Data assimilation technique

Data assimilation technique refers to the method or process used to integrate observational data into a computational model to improve its accuracy and predictive capability. This technique is crucial in fields such as meteorology, oceanography, and environmental science, where it helps to combine real-world measurements with model simulations to produce more reliable and precise results. Describing this property in a research paper highlights the specific approach or algorithm employed to assimilate data, which can be a significant contribution to advancing modeling and forecasting methodologies.
