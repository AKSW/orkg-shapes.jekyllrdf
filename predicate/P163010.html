supported language

It refers to the specific natural languages the model is trained to understand and generate. This property indicates the range of linguistic diversity the model can handle, including its ability to perform tasks such as text generation, translation, or understanding in those languages. A broader set of supported languages enhances the model's utility for global applications and multicultural contexts.
