Abundance

Abundance refers to the quantity or number of a particular element, compound, species, or entity present in a given environment or context. It is a key property used to measure the prevalence, distribution, or concentration of a specific component within a system, which can provide insights into ecological relationships, population dynamics, or the overall health of an ecosystem.
