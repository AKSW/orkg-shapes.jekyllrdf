study limitations

Study limitations refer to the constraints or shortcomings that were acknowledged and discussed in the research study, highlighting areas where the research may have been restricted or where the results may be influenced by certain factors. Understanding and addressing study limitations are important for interpreting the findings accurately and understanding the scope and potential biases of the research.
