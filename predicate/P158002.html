Military Service Risk Factors

The predicate 'Military Service Risk Factors' describes the specific factors or conditions that increase the likelihood of mental ill health and impact functional capability in UK Army Personnel. These risk factors are identified and analyzed in the research paper to understand the gender differences and their effects on military personnel's well-being over their service duration.
