Post Service Risk Factors

Post Service Risk Factors is a research predicate that identifies and explores the factors that contribute to the risk of mental ill health and functional capability in UK Army personnel after their service period. It aims to investigate the specific elements that may impact the well-being and performance of individuals who have served in the UK Army.
