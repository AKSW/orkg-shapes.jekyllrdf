finetuning

Fine-tuning is the process of further training a pre-trained model on a specific task or dataset to improve its performance on that particular task. In the context of LLM-based relation extraction, fine-tuning may refer to the process of adapting a pre-trained model to achieve improved document-level relation extraction performance.
