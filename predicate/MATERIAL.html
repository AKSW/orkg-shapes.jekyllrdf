material

The material refers to the key resources or entities utilized in a research contribution to address the stated research problem. Depending on the domain, materials can take various forms—for example, benchmark datasets in AI (such as ImageNet for image classification) or chemical compounds in material sciences. In the Open Research Knowledge Graph, capturing materials provides insights into the foundational elements that support the research findings.
