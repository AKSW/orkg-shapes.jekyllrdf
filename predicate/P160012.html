Complex Query Handling

Complex Query Handling is the ability to efficiently process and respond to advanced and intricate queries in a given system or database. This research predicate highlights the expertise and innovation demonstrated in addressing complex query scenarios, such as nested queries, optimization techniques, and performance enhancements, within the context of the specified research field.
