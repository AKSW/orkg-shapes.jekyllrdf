quality assessment

Quality assessment refers to the systematic evaluation of the quality, reliability, and validity of research methods, data, or results presented in a scientific study. It involves analyzing the strengths and weaknesses of the research to ensure that the findings are credible and can be trusted. This property is crucial for understanding the rigor and robustness of the research contribution.
