Semantic Reasoning

Semantic Reasoning refers to the ability to infer or derive meaning from information by understanding the relationships between entities, concepts, or symbols. In the context of research, Semantic Reasoning involves using knowledge representation and logical inference to draw conclusions, make decisions, or solve problems based on the semantic meanings of the data or information.
