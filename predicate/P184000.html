paper goal

The 'paper goal' predicate captures the primary objective or aim that the research paper seeks to achieve. It succinctly describes the main purpose or intended outcome of the study, highlighting what the authors set out to investigate, develop, or demonstrate. This property is essential for understanding the core contribution and focus of the research work.
