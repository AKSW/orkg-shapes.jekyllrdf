power plant type

The 'power plant type' predicate specifies the category or classification of a power generation facility discussed or analyzed in the research. This property helps to identify the specific kind of power plant (e.g., coal-fired, nuclear, hydroelectric, solar, wind) that is the focus of the study, enabling a clear understanding of the technological or methodological contributions related to that type of power generation.
