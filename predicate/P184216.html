hydropower scheme type

Hydropower scheme type refers to the classification or category of a hydropower project based on its design, operation, and technology used. This property helps to specify the nature of the hydropower system studied or proposed in the research, such as run-of-river, reservoir-based, pumped storage, or tidal power schemes. Identifying the hydropower scheme type is crucial for understanding the research contribution related to energy generation methods, environmental impact, efficiency, and technological innovation in hydropower development.
