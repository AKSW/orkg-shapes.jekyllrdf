mean (SD) change in function

represents the average change in functional ability or performance of participants over the course of the study, measured in standard deviation units. It reflects the impact of the treatment or intervention on functional outcomes such as activities of daily living or cognitive function. The SD (Standard Deviation) indicates the variability or spread of this change among participants.
