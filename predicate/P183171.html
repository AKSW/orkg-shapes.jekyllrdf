purpose of assimilation

The 'purpose of assimilation' predicate captures the main goal or objective behind the process of assimilation discussed in a research paper. It describes why assimilation is performed or studied, highlighting the intended outcomes, motivations, or aims that drive the assimilation process within the context of the research. This property helps clarify the contribution of the paper by specifying the targeted purpose or rationale for assimilation.
