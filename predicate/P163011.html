knowledge cutoff date

In the context of large language models (LLMs), the "knowledge cutoff date" refers to the latest point in time when the data used for pretraining the model was collected. This property defines the temporal scope of the model's knowledge, indicating that the model may not be aware of events, developments, or information that occurred after this date. It is crucial for understanding the model's limitations in providing up-to-date insights.
