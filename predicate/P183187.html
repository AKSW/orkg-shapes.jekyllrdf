Attitude toward Sustainability

Attitude toward Sustainability refers to the stance, perspective, or approach that individuals, organizations, or societies hold regarding sustainable practices and principles. This property captures how the research addresses, influences, or reflects concerns, values, and commitments related to sustainability, including environmental, social, and economic dimensions. It is used to describe the contribution of the research in promoting, analyzing, or critiquing sustainable development and responsible resource management.
