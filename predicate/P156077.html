actual problems

The predicate 'actual problems' refers to the specific challenges, issues, or obstacles that are addressed or analyzed in the research paper. It highlights the pressing issues or concerns that are the focus of the study and contribute to understanding the impact and relevance of the research in addressing real-world problems.
