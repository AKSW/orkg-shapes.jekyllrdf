literature search representativeness

Literature search representativeness refers to the extent to which the literature search conducted in a research paper comprehensively and accurately reflects the relevant body of existing knowledge on the topic. It indicates how well the selected literature covers the key studies, theories, and findings pertinent to the research question, ensuring that the review is unbiased, thorough, and representative of the field.
