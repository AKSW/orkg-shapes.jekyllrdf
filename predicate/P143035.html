Goodness of fit

Goodness-of-fit is a research predicate that evaluates the degree to which a statistical model fits the data being analyzed. It assesses how well the model predicts the observed outcomes and measures the discrepancy between the predicted values and the actual data points. A high goodness-of-fit indicates that the model accurately captures the relationship between the variables, while a low goodness-of-fit suggests that the model may need improvement or reevaluation.
