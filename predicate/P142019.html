Transparency

Transparency, in a general sense, refers to the openness, clarity, and accessibility of information, processes, or actions. It involves providing clear, comprehensive, and easily understandable information about decisions, operations, or intentions to relevant stakeholders, thereby fostering trust, accountability, and informed decision-making.
