result

The result represents the key findings or outcomes derived from a research contribution after applying a specific method to a given material in addressing the research problem. Results provide evidence of the effectiveness, performance, or implications of the proposed approach and are often expressed in terms of quantitative metrics, qualitative observations, or experimental validations.
