type of evidence

Type of evidence refers to the classification or category of data, observations, or information presented in a research paper that supports the study's findings or conclusions. It helps to understand the nature and strength of the proof provided, such as experimental results, statistical analysis, case studies, simulations, or theoretical arguments.
