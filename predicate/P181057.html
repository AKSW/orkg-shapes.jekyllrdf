science communication criteria

Science communication criteria shows which aspects of science communication are identified/adressed in the paper. It is a Multi-valued field with the following subfields: 1) building trust / credibility 2) enhancing democratic decision-making 3) active participation and collaboration / engagement / interactivity 4) clarity / accessibility 5) increasing knowledge and understanding / awareness 6) accuracy 7) relevance
