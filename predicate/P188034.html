lazy review aspects

lazy thinking heuristics for peer reviews which correspond to superficial, unsupported, or heuristic-based critique patterns that lack proper reasoning or evidence. These aspects are derived from ARR 2022 and EMNLP 2020 reviewing guidelines and are explicitly annotated in the dataset.
