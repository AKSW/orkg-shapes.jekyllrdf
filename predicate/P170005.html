PROM

Patient-Reported Outcome Measures (PROM) is a salient property that highlights the integration of patient-reported assessments, such as symptoms or quality of life, into healthcare practices. This promotes patient-centered care and provides valuable insights into the patient experience and treatment outcomes in the field of Hematology and Oncology.
