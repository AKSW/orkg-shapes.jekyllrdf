main indicators of COVID-19

Death - The total and/or new number of fatalities caused by COVID-19. Active/new cases - Includes total confirmed cases and/or active/new cases, which may or may not include hospitalized patients. Critical and severe cases are considered separately as ancillary indicators. Recovered - Patients who have recovered from the infection Tests - Negative/positive and/or total tests carried out on suspected patients.
