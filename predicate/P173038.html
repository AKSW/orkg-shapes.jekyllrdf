Regulatory land-use exclusion

Regulatory land-use exclusion refers to the restrictions or limitations imposed by government regulations or zoning laws on the use of land for specific purposes, such as prohibiting the development of wind farms in certain areas. This property is identified as a salient factor in the research paper titled 'A global wind farm potential index to increase energy yields and accessibility' to highlight the importance of considering regulatory constraints when assessing the suitability of land for wind energy development.
