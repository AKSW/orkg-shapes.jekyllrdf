literature search method

The 'literature search method' predicate describes the systematic approach or techniques employed by researchers to identify, select, and review relevant academic and scientific literature related to their study. This includes databases searched, keywords used, inclusion and exclusion criteria, and any tools or protocols followed to ensure a comprehensive and unbiased collection of prior work.
